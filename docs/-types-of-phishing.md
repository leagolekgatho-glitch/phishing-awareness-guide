# Types of Phishing

## 1. Email Phishing
Description: this where the criminals send fake emails claiming to be trusted organizations such as banks to trick people into revealing their personal information.

## 2. Spear Phishing
Description: is a targeted form of phishing attack in which an adversary crafts are tailored to a specific individual,or organization, with the intent to fraudulently obtain sensitive information

## 3. Whaling
Description: is a highly targeted form of spear phishing directed specifically at senior or high-profile individuals within an organization such as executives

## 4. Smishing (SMS)
Description: a pishing attack conducted through short message service or other mobile messaging platforms.

## 5. Vishing (Voice calls)
Description:form of phishing through voice communication,typically over a telephone
