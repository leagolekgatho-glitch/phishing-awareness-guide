# What To Do If You Get Phished

## Step 1: Stop
(Don't click more, disconnect from the internet if you opened a file)

## Step 2: Change Passwords
(Use a different device if possible, enable 2FA)

## Step 3: Report
(IT/security team, the impersonated company, local authority)

## Step 4: Scan Your Device
(Run antivirus)

## Step 5: Monitor
(Bank statements, account activity)

## Prevention Tips
- avoid clicking suspicious links
- verify before responding
